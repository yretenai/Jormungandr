# Notes

## Codenames

Bold names denote games that are actively being supported by Jormungandr.
This is that 3-4 letter code after `Data<Platform>_` and what they mean.
Unfortunately there's no real way to know these unless it gets leaked or they choose to let it be known.

- ACB - Brotherhood
- ACGA - Golden Age - Black Flag
- ACFE - Final Episode - Revelations
- ACV - ? - Liberation
- ACU - Unity
- ACC - Comet - Rogue
- ACVI - Victory - Syndicate
- ACE - Empire - Origins
- ACD - Dynasty - Odyssey
- **ACK - Kingdoms - Valhalla**
- **ACRIFT - Rift - Mirage**
- ACRED - Red - Shadows
- POP - Prince of Persia
- POPTM - Prince of Persia ?? - Forgotten Sands
- STP - Steep
- GRN - Grounds - Wildlands
- TGT - Target - Breakpoint
- MAD - Madness - Riders Republic
- **ORP - Orpheus - Immortals Fenyx Rising**
